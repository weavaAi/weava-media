WEAVA Agent – Terms of Service

Last updated: 26 October 2026

WEAVA Agent is an internal tool operated by WEAVA (Linz, Austria). It is used only to publish WEAVA's own videos to WEAVA's own social media accounts (Instagram @weava.ai and TikTok @weava_1) and to read the performance statistics of those posts.

The tool is not offered to the public. No third-party accounts are connected, and no content is posted on behalf of anyone other than WEAVA.

All content published by the tool is created by WEAVA and complies with the terms of each platform. WEAVA may change or stop the tool at any time.
