WEAVA Agent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

WEAVA Agent connects only to WEAVA's own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What data is used

  • Access tokens for WEAVA's own accounts, stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets.
  • Public profile information and aggregate statistics of WEAVA's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, follower count), used to decide what to post and when.

What is not collected

No personal data of other users, followers or visitors is collected, stored, sold or shared. The tool does not read private messages.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept in WEAVA's private repository for content planning. Access can be revoked at any time in the Instagram or TikTok app settings, after which the tokens stop working.
